The mission

The story

You might be where I was 13 years ago.

My son stopped wanting to live, not because of who he was, but because of how I responded to who he was.

He is 23. He is a transgender man. He is an artist in Bilbao. He illustrated this book. He made the cover sculpture. He is the reason any of this exists.

The journey between the father who was certain and the father who understood took thirteen years. This book is the map I did not have.
