Privacy policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

If you give consent, this site uses Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to collect anonymous data about how visitors find and use the site. This includes pages visited, time spent, and general geographic region. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

If you subscribe to the mailing list, we store your name, email address, country, and the relationship you have to a trans person. This data is held in Brevo and used only to send you content you requested.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use tracking pixels from social platforms unless you have given consent.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email ricardo@ricardoramosauthor.com.

To unsubscribe from the mailing list, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send you.

Cookies

We use one consent cookie (ric_consent) to remember your analytics preference. This cookie expires after 365 days. If analytics are accepted, Google Analytics sets additional cookies to distinguish users and sessions. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your cookies.
