Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This policy explains what personal data is collected on ricardoramosauthor.com, why it is collected, how it is used, and what your rights are. This site is operated by Ricardo Ramos, based in Madrid, Spain. It is subject to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spain's Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos (LOPDGDD).

What data is collected and why

The only personal data collected on this site comes from the Chapter 1 request form. When you submit the form, the following information is stored:

  • Name — to personalise the email you receive
  • Email address — to deliver Chapter 1 and occasional emails about the book
  • Country — to understand where readers are and improve future content
  • Your relationship to the situation — to understand who is reading and communicate more relevantly

The legal basis for processing this data is consent. You provide this explicitly when you tick the checkbox on the form. You can withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing.

How data is stored and processed

Your data is stored in Brevo (formerly Sendinblue), an email marketing platform based in France. Brevo complies with GDPR and a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is in place between this site and Brevo.

No data is sold, shared, or passed to any third party for marketing purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not currently use cookies, analytics, or any behavioural tracking. No data about your visit is collected beyond what you voluntarily submit in the form.

If analytics tools are added in future, this policy will be updated and a cookie consent mechanism will be added before any tracking begins.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Unsubscribing removes you from the mailing list immediately. To request full deletion of your data, email ricardo@ricardoramosauthor.com. Deletion requests are honored within 30 days.

Your rights under GDPR

As a resident of the EU or UK, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data held about you
  • Rectification — ask for inaccurate data to be corrected
  • Erasure — ask for your data to be deleted
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent — unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email
  • Lodge a complaint — with Spain's data protection authority, the AEPD, at aepd.es

To exercise any of these rights, contact: ricardo@ricardoramosauthor.com
