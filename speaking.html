The talk

What I got wrong for 42 years that almost killed my son.

"Tu amor de padre puede matar a tu hijo."

I was certain and wrong and it almost killed my son. This talk is about what certainty costs, how inherited beliefs become invisible, and what it actually takes to change a conviction you have held your whole life. Not a story about gender. A story about what happens when a person cannot see past what they already believe.

What happens in the room

01

The certainty trap

How a confident, loving father spent thirteen years absolutely certain he was right, and what that conviction was actually protecting.

02

Inherited beliefs

The beliefs we absorb before we can choose them. Why they feel like truth and behave like identity. Why evidence alone does not move them.

03

The cost of being right

What the data actually shows about what parents do to their children when they prioritise their own certainty. The 82% that changes everything.

04

The mechanics of change

Not inspiration. The specific process by which a person dismantles a core belief and replaces it with something true. With a framework they can use tomorrow.

Where this talk lands

Corporate / Leadership

Beliefs that block leadership

The same mechanism that kept Ricardo from seeing his son clearly operates in every leader who cannot update a conviction in the face of new information. The talk works without ever mentioning gender identity.

DEI & inclusion

The parent in the workplace

Forty-five percent of transgender youth seriously consider suicide. Most of them have a parent in your organisation. This talk addresses the people who actually hold the power in those families.
