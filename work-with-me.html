A private 30-minute conversation.

This is not a sales call. It is a conversation. You tell me where you are. I tell you what I know. We decide together if there is more to do.

I have been exactly where you are. I am not a therapist and I will not pretend to be one. I am a father who spent thirteen years getting this wrong, then found his way through. That is the only thing I am offering.

Who this call is for

Parent

You have just found out, or you have known for a while and done nothing. You do not know where to start and you are not ready to tell anyone else.

Spouse or partner

Your partner is not ready. Or you are not ready. Or you are both somewhere different. You need to talk to someone who understands that gap from the inside.

Father specifically

You feel something that is hard to name. It is not hate. It is not rejection. But it is not acceptance either. You want to understand what is happening to you.

This call is not therapy.

I am not a therapist and I cannot replace one. If you are in crisis, if your child is in danger, or if what you are carrying requires professional clinical support, I will tell you that directly and help you find the right resource. What I offer is the experience of a father who made every mistake and came out the other side. Sometimes that is exactly what is needed. Sometimes it is not. We will know quickly which one applies.
